In this example, 304 staff hours are freed up each year.

Example assumptions

  • Current annual hours: 364 (12 minutes × 35 times a week × 52 weeks ÷ 60).
  • Assumed hours saved: 304 a year (364 current − 60 after improvement).
  • Hourly cost: NZD $60, giving an annual value of NZD $18,240 (304 × $60).

Illustrative figures only. Setup and ongoing software costs are excluded. Time freed up does not automatically reduce cash costs.
